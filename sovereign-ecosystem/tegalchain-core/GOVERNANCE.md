# GOVERNANCE

Tegalchain operates as a DAO with role-based responsibilities and a merit-weighted voting system.

Roles
- Contributor: submits work and evidence
- Reviewer: reviews PRs and evidence
- Maintainer: merges changes and manages releases
- Council: elected body for high-level decisions and treasury governance

Voting & treasury
- **TEGAL** (micro-denom **uteg**) is the native governance and utility token — never pre-mined. Acquired only through impact-gated block creation, exchange purchase, and DAO-governed burn technology. See [tegal-tokens.md](./docs/tegal-tokens.md) and the sovereign-ecosystem [TEGAL-TOKENS contract](https://github.com/Shootle/sovereign-ecosystem/blob/main/docs/contracts/TEGAL-TOKENS.md).
- **Transynths (tX***)** represent environmentally provenance impact assets (PoREX) — distinct from TEGAL.
- **Voixcoin** — stable value minted only against **PA (Provenance Assets)** locked in the custodianless Greenledger. See [VOIXCOIN contract](https://github.com/Shootle/sovereign-ecosystem/blob/main/docs/contracts/VOIXCOIN.md).
- Treasury allocations are proposed and voted monthly.

Dispute resolution
- Community mediation and a Council appeal process. See CONTRIBUTING.md for escalation.
