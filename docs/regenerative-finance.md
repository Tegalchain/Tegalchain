# Regenerative Finance Overview

Tegalchain measures regenerative impact with RFM (Regenerative Finance Metrics): EQS (Evidence Quality Score), RIV (Regenerative Impact Value), and Consensus Confidence. These metrics feed PoRE/PoREF decisions and PoREX market valuations.

**Transitional Synthetic Assets (Transynths)**, digitally represented as **tX*** (where * indexes environmentally provenance asset types), represent synthetic, verifiable units of impact (see PoREX docs). The platform incentivizes high-quality evidence and long-term regenerative outcomes.
